## Deviations from the spec (for review)
- `deferred` close jobs do NOT make the user busy for the guardian (spec 19.6 says pending/deferred). A deferred job
  waits for the recovery lane, and the lane skips busy users, so counting it would deadlock until the 24 h expiry.
- The entry duplicate check uses an ORM pre-check plus `IntegrityError` on the partial UNIQUE index instead of
  `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING RETURNING`; same outcome, and the v1 path stays the original ORM insert.
- `CLOSE_JOB_MAX_ATTEMPTS` fails a job only on a not-flat close (residual or a rejected close order); read errors
  and failed TP cancels retry until the 24 h expiry.
